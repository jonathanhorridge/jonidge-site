Delete your Gust Rally data

Gust Rally has no accounts. Here is where your data is and how to delete it.

On your device

Your progress, Breeze, cosmetics, streak, quests and settings are stored only on your device. Delete them by clearing the app's data (Android: Settings, Apps, Gust Rally, Storage, Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. We cannot see or delete this data for you.

Online leaderboard and ghosts (if you used them)

The online player record (random ID, display name, friend code), your daily runs (ghosts) and your follows are deleted automatically: runs after 45 days, and players without runs after 60 days. To delete them immediately, use Settings, Delete online data in the app, where it is offered. Or email jonidge@yahoo.com from any address, tell us your display name and friend code (shown in the app), and we will delete the record within 30 days. We cannot find a player without that information because we store no email or device ID.

Purchases and ads

Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat (see the Privacy Policy) and for bookkeeping as the law requires. Ad data is held by Google; manage it at https://myadcenter.google.com.

Support emails

Write to jonidge@yahoo.com to have emails you sent us deleted.
