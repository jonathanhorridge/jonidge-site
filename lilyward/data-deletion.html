Lilyward by Jonidge

Privacy Terms Support Delete your data

Lilyward does not create a player account. Almost all of your data lives on the device you play on.

Delete the save on your device

  • iPhone or iPad: delete the Lilyward app. This removes the local save. You can also offload or delete the app from iOS Settings.
  • Android: delete the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Lilyward → Storage → Clear data.
  • Browser: clear this site’s data (or cookies and site storage) for jonidge.com in your browser settings. That wipes flies, pearls, hats and level progress stored there.

Purchases and ads

Buying something is recorded by Apple or Google (and, in the native app, by RevenueCat). Uninstalling the game does not cancel a subscription-style record of a non-consumable purchase; the store still knows you paid, which is what lets Restore Purchases work. To ask those companies to delete identifiers they hold, use their own privacy tools, or email us and we will point you to the right form.

If ads have been enabled, Google AdMob may have an advertising identifier for your device. You can reset that identifier in your device settings, and you can contact us for help with a deletion request.

Ask us

Email jonidge@yahoo.com with the subject “Lilyward data deletion”. Tell us which platform you used (web, iOS or Android). We will confirm what we can delete and what you need to clear on the device or with the store. We aim to reply within 30 days.
