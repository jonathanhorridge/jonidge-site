Privacy policy

Lilyward by Jonidge

Privacy Terms Support Delete your data

This policy explains what Lilyward (“the game”), published by Jonidge, does with your information. The game is made for phones and also plays in a web browser. It does not ask you to create an account.

If we need a postal address: Jonidge, Rue du Jura 4, 1043 Sugnens, Switzerland. Email: jonidge@yahoo.com.

Information stored on your device

Progress (levels, stars, flies, pearls, hats, settings and whether you have removed ads) is saved only on your device, using the browser’s local storage or the app’s private storage. We do not have a player account or a server that keeps a copy of that save.

Advertising (when ads are turned on)

The game may show optional rewarded videos (for extra flies or pearls, a second chance, or double flies) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may collect a device advertising identifier, coarse location, IP address, and technical information about your device in order to show ads and to measure them. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) so you can accept or refuse personalised ads, as required by applicable law.

You can also buy Remove Ads, which turns off the between-level ads. Optional reward videos remain available if you want them.

Until ads are enabled in a store release, the game does not contact an ad network.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, a starter pack, and pearl packs) are processed by Apple, Google, or — in a native app — RevenueCat acting as our purchase helper. Those companies process the payment and a record of what you bought. We do not see your card number. Restore Purchases on the Shop screen re-applies non-consumable items (Remove Ads and the starter pack) that you already paid for on the same store account.

Analytics and accounts

Lilyward does not use a separate analytics SDK and does not require a login. There is no chat, no social graph and no user-generated public content.

Children

The game is a mild cartoon strategy game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads, when enabled, are requested as non-personalised in regions where consent is required and has not been given.

How long we keep data

The save file stays on your device until you clear the site data, delete the app, or use the in-game reset if one is provided. Ad and purchase records are kept by Google, Apple or RevenueCat under their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Switzerland and the EU/EEA), you may ask us to confirm what we hold, to correct it, or to delete it. Because we do not run player accounts, most of this is done on your device — see Delete your data. For anything held by an ad or store partner, email jonidge@yahoo.com and we will help as far as we can.

Partners’ policies

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple and Google’s store privacy policies, which apply to payments made through their stores
