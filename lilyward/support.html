A short how-to

  • Place frogs on lily pads beside the path to stop bugs reaching the frog eggs.
  • Each frog does a different job. Mixing types is stronger (and cheaper) than stacking one kind.
  • Upgrade or sell a frog by tapping it. Selling gives back 70% of what you spent on it.
  • Send the next wave early for bonus flies (coins in the level).
  • Fill the CROAK meter and tap it to stun every bug at once.
  • Three worlds — Willow Pond, Cattail Creek and Moonlit Fen — unlock in order. Stars on a level give flies and pearls for the shop.

Purchases and ads

If a purchase did not appear, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you used to buy. For a refund, use the App Store or Google Play refund process; we cannot take card payments ourselves.

Progress disappeared

Progress is saved only on that device (or browser). Clearing the site’s data, or uninstalling the app without a backup, deletes it. We cannot remotely restore a local save.
