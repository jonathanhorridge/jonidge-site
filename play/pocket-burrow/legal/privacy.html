Pocket Burrow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-28
Last updated: 2026-11-28

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you play Pocket Burrow (the "App") on Android, iOS or in a web browser, why, and what your choices are. We kept it short and plain. If anything is unclear, write to us at the address in section 1.

The short version

  • You don't create an account. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your burrow, critters, album, Acorns, Glowberries, decorations and settings are stored on your device. The App has no game server and no online features.
  • If rewarded ads are switched on in your version, they are optional "sunny breaks" from Google AdMob. You choose to watch one for a small stated reward, at most 5 a day. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first.
  • If you turn on gentle reminders, your phone schedules them on the device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Optional purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

2. What data is processed, why, and on what legal basis

2.1 Game data stored on your device

WhatWhyWhere
Your burrow (dug tunnels, rooms and their levels), critters met, residents and friendship hearts, album, Acorns, goods, Glowberries, decorations, daily-gift and season progress, visitor times, settings (sound, music, seasons, reminders, reduced motion), the day's rewarded-ad counters, and the IDs of store purchases already delivered (so nothing is delivered twice)To run the gameOnly on your device: the operating system's app storage in the App, or your browser's local storage on the web. The one-time Supporter pack can be restored where the store supports it. The optional "Back-up code" in Settings is a text code that only you see and keep; the App doesn't send it anywhere. Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

2.2 Gentle reminders (optional, off by default)

If you turn on reminders, the App asks your phone for permission to show notifications. It then schedules up to four local reminders (for example "a visitor is at your door"), never at night. Your device's operating system creates and shows them. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the App or in your phone's settings at any time.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob), only if switched on

The App may offer optional rewarded video ads ("sunny breaks"). You choose to watch one for a reward we state first: doubling one basket, halving a digging timer, ringing the Wanderer's bell, or one Glowberry. Ads are never required to play, are capped at 5 a day, and are limited to General-audience content. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. Ads are served by Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) and Google LLC (elsewhere). When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device identifiers and sends them to Google, together with your IP address (approximate location), ad interaction data, diagnostics and basic device information. Device identifiers include the advertising ID where it is available and allowed. Where the law requires it, the App first shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) and personalises ads only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. On iOS the advertising ID is accessed only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt. You get the same rewards either way.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and ePrivacy rules) for personalised ads and identifiers. Otherwise, our legitimate interest in financing a free app and preventing ad fraud (Art. 6(1)(f)). Google acts as an independent controller: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. In versions where ads are switched off, no ad data is collected.

2.4 Purchases (Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat), only if switched on

Optional purchases (baskets of Glowberries and a one-time "Cozy Supporter" pack) are paid only through Apple's App Store or Google Play billing. Apple or Google processes your payment as an independent controller, and we never receive your card number, bank details or billing address. To deliver and verify purchases, we receive data from the store and process it through RevenueCat, Inc. (USA, our processor). This covers a random anonymous app user ID, purchase records and basic technical data. Purchase records include product, price, currency, store country, date, transaction ID, receipt or purchase token, and refund status. Technical data includes app version, operating system, device type and locale. The stores also send us sales reports for accounting and tax. Purposes: delivering what you bought, restoring purchases, fraud prevention, refunds, support and bookkeeping. Legal basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)) and fraud prevention (Art. 6(1)(f)). RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

2.5 Consent records

If ads are on, Google's User Messaging Platform stores your privacy choices on your device (for example the IAB TCF consent string) so the App can respect them. Legal basis: the legal obligation to obtain and document consent (Art. 6(1)(c), Art. 7(1)).

2.6 Support emails

If you email us, we process your email address, your message and anything you attach in order to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of the contract where it concerns a purchase (Art. 6(1)(b)). Our support mailbox is a Yahoo Mail account provided by Yahoo International Limited (The EXO Building, North Wall Quay, Dublin 1, Ireland), which stores your emails for us. Yahoo may process data in other countries, including the US, under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (https://legal.yahoo.com/ie/en/yahoo/privacy/index.html).

2.7 Playing in a web browser

The web version at jonidge.com is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When your browser loads the pages, GitHub processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the site and keep it secure, under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. The web version uses no cookies, no analytics, no real ads and no real purchases. Any ads or purchases you see there are clearly marked simulations. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

2.8 What we do not do

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own. We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We have no user accounts, emails or passwords, no chat and no contact between players. We do not make decisions with legal effects about you by automated means.

6. Children

Pocket Burrow is a gentle, general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, but it has no content that is unsuitable for them. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and no personal details are needed to play. Ads, where switched on, are limited to General-audience content. Parents can manage purchases with Apple's Screen Time / Ask to Buy and Google Play's Family Link. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email jonidge@yahoo.com and we will delete it.

7. Security

The App has no server of its own and keeps your game on your device. Connections to Google, Apple and RevenueCat are encrypted (HTTPS). We keep as little data as possible. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, and you can object to processing. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect earlier processing. Because most data stays on your device, you can delete it yourself. Anything else can be deleted by email: see Delete data. Contact us at jonidge@yahoo.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Switzerland, that is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

9. Notice for US residents (California and other states)

We do not sell your personal information for money. Where ads are on, Google's use of identifiers to personalise ads may count as "sharing" or "selling" under some state laws. You can opt out in the App (Settings > Privacy choices) or in your device's ad settings, or by emailing jonidge@yahoo.com. Categories processed in the last 12 months: identifiers (advertising ID and IP address where ads are on, and an anonymous purchase user ID where purchases are on), commercial information (purchase records where purchases are on) and internet activity (ad interactions where ads are on). We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
