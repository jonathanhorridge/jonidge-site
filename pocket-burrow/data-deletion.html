Delete your Pocket Burrow data

Pocket Burrow has no accounts and no game server. Here is where your data is and how to delete it.

On your device

Your burrow, critters, album, Acorns, Glowberries, decorations and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them, use More > Start over in the App, clear the App's data (Android: Settings > Apps > Pocket Burrow > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the App. On the web, clear the site data for jonidge.com in your browser. We cannot see or delete this data for you.

Reminders

Scheduled reminders are deleted when you turn them off, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Purchases and ads

Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat (see the Privacy Policy) and, where the law requires it, for bookkeeping. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record, email jonidge@yahoo.com with the order ID from your store receipt, and we will make the request within 30 days. Ad data is held by Google; manage it at https://myadcenter.google.com, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Support emails

Write to jonidge@yahoo.com to have the emails you sent us deleted.
