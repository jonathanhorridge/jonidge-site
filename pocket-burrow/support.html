Pocket Burrow support

Email: jonidge@yahoo.com

Please tell us your device model and operating system, the app version (shown under More), and what happened. For a purchase problem, include the order ID from your Apple or Google receipt. We usually reply within a few working days.

Quick answers

  • Where is my burrow saved? On your device only. To move it to a new phone, use More > Back-up code on the old phone, then Restore on the new one.
  • Why did nobody visit? Visitors arrive at set times of day (morning, afternoon, evening). Building more room types invites more kinds of critters. The note at the top of the screen tells you when the next one comes.
  • A purchase didn't arrive. Open the Nook again with an internet connection. The one-time Supporter pack can be restored under More > Restore purchases. If it still hasn't arrived, email us your order ID.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).
  • Ads and privacy: sunny breaks are always optional. You can change your ad privacy choice under More > Privacy choices (where offered).
  • Reminders: turn them on or off under More > Gentle reminders. There are never more than four, and never at night.
  • Ads and purchases may be switched off in your version.
