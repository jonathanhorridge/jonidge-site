Delete your data

Rubber Band Bridge by Jonidge

Rubber Band Bridge has no accounts, and we don't keep your game data on any server. Everything the game saves (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  1. In the game: Settings → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. This erases all progress and coins on that device.
  2. Android: Settings → Apps → Rubber Band Bridge → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  3. iOS: uninstall the app (press and hold the icon → Remove App → Delete App).
  4. Web version: clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising data: ads in the mobile apps are served by Google AdMob, which processes its own data as described in our Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), and manage Google data at My Ad Center.

Purchase records are kept by Google Play or Apple as required by law, not by us.

Questions or a deletion request? Email jonidge@yahoo.com and we will reply within 30 days. Deleted coins and progress cannot be restored.
