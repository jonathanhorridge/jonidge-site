Privacy Policy

Rubber Band Bridge (Android, iOS and web). Effective 28 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The controller for the app is Jonidge, Rue du Jura 4, 1043 Sugnens, Switzerland. Email: jonidge@yahoo.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The mobile apps show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You decide when to watch one.
  • Coin packs are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • The web version on jonidge.com has no ads, no purchases, no cookies and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

The game saves its state in your device's local storage: level progress, stars, coin balance, hints bought, ad count for the day, and sound and music settings. This data does not leave your device and we cannot access it. You can erase it at any time (see Delete your data).

Advertising (mobile apps only)

The mobile apps include Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, to offer optional rewarded ads. Ads only play when you tap "Watch an ad". The AdMob SDK starts when the app opens (after the consent step below) so an ad is ready when you want one. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language)
  • your IP address (used to estimate a rough location)
  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where you allow it) and app- or developer-scoped IDs such as Android's app set ID
  • diagnostic and performance data (crash logs, app launch time, hang rate, energy use)
  • product interactions (app launches, taps, ads shown, video views)

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or no consent. You can change your choice later in Settings → "Ad privacy". On iOS the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, your IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with it.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Legal basis: your consent where required (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), Swiss FADP), otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free game through optional ads (Art. 6(1)(f)).

In-app purchases (mobile apps only)

Coin packs are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms and privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded (a transaction identifier and the product bought), which the app uses on your device to add the coins. We do not receive or store your name, email, payment card or billing address.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social logins.
  • We don't sell personal data.
  • We don't run our own servers that receive data from the game.

Children

Rubber Band Bridge is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with a "general audience" maximum content rating. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you reset the game, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside Switzerland and the EEA, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US / Swiss–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Swiss FADP or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no personal data about you on our side, most requests are handled on your device or with Google directly. Write to jonidge@yahoo.com and we will help. You may also complain to your data protection authority. In Switzerland that is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
