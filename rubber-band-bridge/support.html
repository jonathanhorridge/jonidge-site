Rubber Band Bridge: Support

Need help? Email jonidge@yahoo.com. Please include your device model, operating system version, the app version (shown in the store listing) and the level number if it's about a level.

How to play

Drag from one gold peg to another to stretch a band, then press GO. Pip walks right by himself. Green bands are bridges, yellow bands bounce Pip when he drops onto them, and red bands snap under him. A hard fall onto a red band launches him upward. Fewer bands earn more stars.

Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck on a level. Tap Help. A hint lights up the next band of the answer, and every 3 failed tries on a level gives you a free hint. You can also buy an extra band or have the whole solution placed for you with coins.

How do I get coins? Clear levels (first clears pay most), watch an optional ad (up to 5 a day), or buy a coin pack in the app.

I bought coins but didn't get them. Close and reopen the app while online. Completed purchases are credited when the store reports them. If it still doesn't work, email us with the order number from your store receipt. For refunds, use Google Play's or Apple's refund process.

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on the device. It's lost if the app's data is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or Settings → Reset progress is used. It doesn't sync between devices.

How do I change my ad choices? Settings → Ad privacy (shown where it applies). On iOS you can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Can I turn off sound or music? Yes. Use Settings or the pause menu.
