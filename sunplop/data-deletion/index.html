Delete your Sunplop data

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Sunplop does not have user accounts, logins or cloud saves. There is no account to close. This page explains how to delete the game data on your device and how to ask us to delete information held by us or our providers.

This page is the public instruction page for data-deletion and account-deletion requests (Apple and Google). Because the app has no accounts, the stores do not require an in-app “delete account” button; the steps below still let you erase what we can erase.

1. Delete game data on your device

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & ads → Delete game data. Confirm the warning. This erases your progress, Rays, stars, streak, Jokers, settings and other game data stored on that device. It cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage in your device settings, also removes this local data.

We cannot see, restore or delete this data for you, because it never leaves your device.

Purchases: Rays (including purchased Rays) are stored only on your device and are lost when you delete game data or uninstall. One-time purchases that the store can restore, such as No ads, come back with Settings → Restore purchases after you reinstall. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

2. Ask us to delete data held off the device

Some information may be held outside your device:

  • Purchase-verification records at our provider RevenueCat (an anonymous app user ID, product, price and receipt data — not your card or bank details).
  • Support emails if you have written to us (held in our Yahoo Mail support mailbox).
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob (see the Privacy Policy). We cannot delete Google’s advertising records ourselves; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

To request deletion of purchase-verification records and support emails, write to jonidge@yahoo.com with:

  • the word “deletion” in the subject,
  • your device (iPhone or Android) and app version (Settings → About),
  • your support ID if the app shows one (Settings → About, or the pre-filled support email), and/or
  • the store order ID (Google Play IDs start with “GPA.”; Apple IDs are on your receipt email).

We will not ask for more information than we need to find the records. We answer within 30 days (one month under the GDPR). We may keep a limited record of the request and of purchases where the law requires it (for example accounting and tax rules).

3. No account to delete

Sunplop does not let you create an account. If a store or a form asks for an “account deletion” URL, this page is that URL: there is no account, and the steps above are how you delete your data.
