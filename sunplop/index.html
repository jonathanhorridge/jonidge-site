About the game

Sunplop is a calm, clever logic puzzle about placing suns.

The rules fit in one breath

  • Put exactly 2 suns in every row and every column.
  • A numbered tile is never a sun. Its number tells you how many suns touch it, diagonals included.
  • That’s it. Tap once for a sun, twice for a dot, and let logic do the rest.

A new puzzle every day

The Daily Sun is the same puzzle for every player, every day. Solve it to keep your streak alive, then share your result with friends without spoiling the solution.

2,400+ levels that grow with you

Start with quick 4×4 grids that teach you the basics, then climb through 100 chapters up to big 9×9 boards. New twists appear along the way:

  • Clouds: tiles that are never suns and count nothing.
  • Green numbers: they only count the 4 tiles directly beside them.
  • Bigger grids that reward careful thinking.

Fair, checked puzzles

Every level and every daily puzzle has been checked by a solver to have exactly one solution, so you never get stuck on a broken puzzle.

Play your way

  • Earn up to 3 stars per level: solve without hints and beat the target time.
  • Collect Rays as you play and spend them on hints, skipping a tough level, or a Streak Joker that saves a missed day.
  • Undo, clear and hints whenever you need them.
  • Sound effects and relaxing music, both easy to switch off.
  • No account needed.
  • Available in English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Sunplop is free to play. It contains ads and offers optional in-app purchases, including Ray packs and removing banner ads.

Good luck, and enjoy the sunshine!
