Common questions

My Rays are gone after reinstalling. Rays and progress are stored only on your device. We don't have accounts, so we can't see or restore them. One-time purchases like "No ads" come back with Settings → Restore purchases.

I bought something and didn't get it. Open the shop again with an internet connection; purchases are delivered once the store confirms them. If it's still missing, email us with the order ID.

Refunds. Refunds are handled by the store: Apple → https://reportaproblem.apple.com · Google Play → https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

Ads and privacy choices. Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage privacy choices (EU/UK/Switzerland) or "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" (US). Buying "No ads" removes banner and between-level ads; optional reward videos stay.

Delete my data. Settings → Privacy & ads → Delete game data erases everything on your device. To delete purchase records held by our purchase-verification provider, email us your support ID (shown in the support email template) or order ID. Full instructions: Delete your data.
